02 — Selected work

What I built this year

Six projects from first year. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

Escaping Santa

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 1 month

A Christmas Themed Parkour Platformer game written in Blueprints. You are a penguin who must save their last 5 family members from Santa and then make your escape, while also avoiding all the dangers that Santa has sent after you.

My role: everything — gameplay classes, spawning, scoring, HUD and the end-of-round state.

UBIYTSA Indoor Level Creation

Unreal Engine 5 . Dungeon Scrawl · Level design · Solo · 2-3 days

An indoor stealth level built to a brief: one route to the objective which is to find the secret entrance to the underground bunker. With a design based on a royalty building, this level's environment, combined with it's combat and puzzles. Provides an interesting playstyle for the players to experience.

My role: layout, pacing, sightlines, playtest notes and Level Design.

Wooden Chair Asset

Blender · Substance Painter · Solo · 2 days

One medium sized low-poly Wooden Chair Asset, made with under 1,000 Triangles, made from scratch, UV unwrapped, textured, and imported into Unreal with correct scale and collision, This asset simple provided more decoration into one of my projects.

My role: modelling, UVs, texturing and engine import.

Music/Sound Blueprints

Reaper · Unreal MetaSounds · Solo · 1 week

Royalty free music that I found and added to my first Unreal Engine Project. I added this music because I believe it fit my game very well. Having the player run around in a maze looking for coins so they can get to school.

My role: recording, editing, and implementation in engine.

WeaponWheel Blueprint

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 2 weeks

A WeaponWheel that I made for my 3rd Game project. I made this WeaponWheel because of how many weapons me and my partner Tiarnan planned to add to our Stealth Genre type game. So we believed a WeaponWheel was the best option to be able to fit all the weapons we wanted, into our Stealth game, that's similar to Dishonored.

My role: WeaponWheel system, Widgets, asset creation, and Character Blueprints.

Gameplay Of 1st Game Project

Unreal Engine 5/Blender/Adobe . Blueprints · Solo · 2 months

My 1st Game Project was a 3D environmental maze runner combined with a playstyle similar to flappybird. In this game you are late to school and are tasked in finding 5 gold coins duguised as your lunch money. You need to hurry though because the money is scattered around the maze and you only have 5 minutes until your offically late and it's game over. This image simple shows gameplay of my first ever game project during my level 3 Game Development course at Belfast Met.

My role: gameplay programming, mechanic development, level designing, character blueprints, widgets, asset creation and build packaging.

03 — About

About me

I am a second-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I came to this course from my neverending passion for games and my desire to becoming a Game Developer myself, and so I came here to learn how to build them properly.

I am most interested in gameplay programming — the systems that decide how something feels to control and level design/creation as it feels very natural and fun to me. This year I have learned how to and worked in C++ and Blueprints, modelled and textured my own assets, and recorded and implemented my own audio.

Next I want to go deeper into C++ and take on a longer team project. And then look into getting a higher education in Game's Development by going to University.
